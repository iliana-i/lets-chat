The ask

Three parallel tracks in two months, as first priority, while still shipping two other ongoing projects:

  1. A custom agent builder UI for non-technical users — ready to launch to customers at our yearly conference.
  2. Agentic as a whole new market — customers needed to be able to buy the agent solution without buying the rest of the iPaaS platform, so we could price it competitively as a standalone AI offering, reusing as much internal infrastructure as possible.
  3. An immediate rollout to 2–4 early partners — making the hacked-together solution as usable as possible, as-is, in the meantime.

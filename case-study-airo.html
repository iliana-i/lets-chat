The ask

Alongside Agent Studio, Workato needed a second AI flagship: a Gen AI assistant embedded across the platform that could help builders describe a business process in natural language and get a working orchestration—planning steps, generating recipes, and monitoring runs—without breaking the patterns, connections, and enterprise guardrails the core product was built on.

My job: lead design end to end for AIRO, set interaction patterns for Gen AI across the platform, and ship a 0→1 experience fast enough to put in front of early adopters while the underlying AI capabilities were still evolving weekly.
